# Community

Join the SanityOps community. Get hands-on with the tools, explore worked samples and FAQs, learn about the project, and connect with other practitioners.

## Resources

- [Try the Tools](/framework/try-the-tools) — Openness overview and tooling entry points: the open-source inspection skill and Inspect CLI (Inspect + Risk Explicit), plus the Risk Implicit and Quality SaaS demos and self-hosted deployment options
- [Samples](/samples/) — Worked Logic Artifacts and defect inspection reports produced by the SanityOps tools
- [FAQ](/framework/FAQ) — Answers to common questions for AI service managers, developers, operators, and security teams, with sources cited from the official specifications

---

**SanityOps is open by design**: the framework specification is open under CC BY-SA 4.0, and two inspection tools are open source — an inspection skill (CC BY-SA 4.0) and the Inspect CLI (Apache 2.0); the broader Platform is offered commercially as SaaS or self-hosted.
