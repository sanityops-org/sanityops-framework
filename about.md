# About SanityOps

**SanityOps** is an independent, vendor-neutral, documentation-first framework for AI Agent governance. It is not a commercial product, a startup, or an initiative backed by any single vendor. It is a specification-driven methodology designed to be implemented, adapted, and extended by any team or organization.

## What SanityOps Is

- A governance framework that systematically inspects, validates, and assesses AI Agent logic artifacts — System Prompts, Skills, and Tool Schemas — across their full lifecycle.
- A vendor-neutral specification: the framework defines _what_ to check and _how_ to reason about results, without prescribing a specific tool stack.
- A documentation-first project: the specifications are the primary artifact. Reference tooling may complement but does not replace the written methodology.

## What SanityOps Is Not

- Not a commercial platform or SaaS product.
- Not affiliated with any single cloud provider, model vendor, or AI platform.
- Not a replacement for existing observability, IAM, or production operations tooling.

## Positioning

SanityOps occupies a layer that sits between raw AI Agent development and production governance. It does not compete with evaluation libraries, guardrail solutions, or monitoring platforms — it provides the governance rationale that connects them.

The framework is structured around four interconnected subsets: **Inspect** (defect discovery), **Risk** (security validation), **Quality** (service assessment), and **Relevance** (impact mapping). Together they form a governance closed loop that is inspectable, evidencable, and regression-verifiable.
