## Building Governable AI Agents Together

Enterprise AI agents are becoming more capable. But for many organizations,
the ability to govern them has not kept pace.

SanityOps is a management-facing, platform-level governance framework for
enterprise AI agents. It is not merely another prompt tool, quality evaluation
tool, or security scanner. It provides a structured way to inspect logical
artifacts, validate risk, assess service quality, and retain evidence across
the agent lifecycle.[^6a6c710237ee18b094b414a4_16]

The goal is not to promise that an agent will always be correct or completely
secure. It is to reduce uncertainty and business risk through continuous,
version-aware checks, validation, regression, and evidence governance.[^6a6a0bda577038d7bd5d2359_13]

## A Governance Layer for Enterprise AI Delivery

SanityOps does not build, orchestrate, or operate agents. It provides
systematic inspection, audit, and quality measurement for the logical artifacts
and results of agents that have already been built.[^6a6c710237ee18b094b414a4_28]

For Forward-Deployed Engineering teams, this creates a practical governance
layer for delivering high-quality enterprise AI services.

FDE teams are responsible for making AI work within real customer workflows,
constraints, and delivery expectations. SanityOps helps them make that work
more governable:

- Inspect prompts, skills, tool schemas, and cross-artifact logic;
- Validate explicit risks and investigate implicit risks;
- Assess service quality through repeatable methods;
- Preserve evidence for review, release decisions, iteration, and regression;
- Establish a clearer governance baseline across customer engagements.

The objective is not only to build an agent that works in a demo, but to help
deliver an AI service that can be inspected, validated, improved, and governed
over time.

## Who We Work With

### FDE & Enterprise AI Delivery Teams

For teams deploying AI agents into enterprise environments, SanityOps provides
a governance foundation that can be incorporated into delivery methods,
acceptance processes, and ongoing iteration.

### AI Platforms & Agent Builders

For agent platforms, orchestration tools, enterprise AI middleware, and
vertical AI products, SanityOps can serve as an independent governance layer.

We are open to integration, embedded deployment, OEM, and joint-solution
discussions where governance needs to coexist with existing agent-building and
operational capabilities.

### Governance & Audit Partners

SanityOps Framework is fully open and designed around explicit objects,
findings, risk levels, quality results, and evidence requirements.

This makes it a useful open reference for organizations seeking to establish
repeatable AI agent governance and audit practices—not as a claimed industry
standard, but as a foundation from which enterprises, industries, and
professional service teams can develop their own governance baselines, review
methods, and acceptance criteria.

### Design Partners

We work with organizations building high-value, complex, or high-risk AI agent
systems and looking to make governance practical in real delivery environments.

Several large enterprise teams are currently evaluating SanityOps through POC
engagements. Due to confidentiality, we do not disclose their identities,
industries, or implementation details.

## Working Alongside Existing Controls

SanityOps does not replace IAM, infrastructure security, data governance,
production monitoring, or model fine-tuning. It works alongside these systems
by strengthening governance over the agent logic that organizations can
directly control: prompts, skills, tool schemas, and their relationships.[^6a6c710237ee18b094b414a4_32]
