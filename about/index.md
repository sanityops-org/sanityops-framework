# About

**SanityOps is an open, vendor-neutral continuous governance framework for AI agents. Learn about its origin, its mission, and how to join the work.**

---

## Two Sections

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- ### [About SanityOps](./about.md)

    The full origin story, what we learned, what we build, and what we don't do.

    Covers the framework's founding insight — that quality and safety are the same question for AI agents — and why SanityOps treats **Inspect**, **Risk**, and **Quality** as one connected discipline. Includes an honest boundary table: what SanityOps is not.

    **Open by design**: core specifications and governance logic are open source under CC BY-SA 4.0.

- ### [Partnership](./partnership.md)

    Building governable AI agents together.

    Explores how SanityOps serves as a governance layer for enterprise AI delivery — not by building agents, but by providing systematic inspection, audit, and quality measurement for the artifacts and results of agents that have already been built.

    Covers who we work with: FDE teams, AI platforms, governance and audit partners, and design partners.

</div>
