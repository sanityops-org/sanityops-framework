# Community

SanityOps is a community-driven framework. Participation is open to practitioners, researchers, and organizations.

## Ways to Participate

- **Specification review**: Review and provide feedback on framework specifications, defect patterns, and assessment methodologies.
- **Implementation feedback**: Share experiences, challenges, and results from applying the framework in real-world Agent deployments.
- **Use case discussion**: Discuss application scenarios, edge cases, and domain-specific adaptations with other practitioners and contributors.
- **Regional and language adaptations**: Contribute translations, regional case studies, or adaptations for local regulatory and operational contexts.
